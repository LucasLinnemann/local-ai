# Northwind Tooling PTO Policy

## Accrual

Full-time employees receive 17 PTO days per year. Days are credited on
January 1. Employees hired mid-year receive a prorated amount based on
their start month.

## Requesting time off

PTO requests must be submitted 9 days in advance through the HR portal.
Requests for more than 5 consecutive days need approval from the
department manager. Requests during the last two weeks of December are
approved in the order they are received.

## Carryover

Unused PTO days do not carry over to the next year. Employees are
reminded in October of their remaining balance. There is no payout for
unused days, except at termination where state law requires it.

## Sick time

Sick time is separate from PTO. Employees receive 6 sick days per year.
A doctor's note is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.
