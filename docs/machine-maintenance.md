# Northwind Tooling Machine Maintenance Guide

## Coolant

Coolant is changed every 30 days. Concentration is checked weekly with a
refractometer and should read between 7 and 9 percent. Low coolant is
topped up with premixed solution, never plain water.

## Spindle

The spindle torque spec is 45 newton meters. Spindle bearings are
inspected every 2,000 run hours. Any vibration reading above 4 mm/s
means the machine is taken out of service until the bearings are checked.

## Filters

Air filters are replaced every 90 days. Coolant filters are replaced
every 60 days or sooner if flow drops. Used filters go in the marked
bin by the maintenance cage.

## Carryover

Maintenance tasks not finished in a shift carry over to the next shift
and are written on the handoff board. The incoming shift lead signs off
on each carried task.
